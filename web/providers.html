Using a provider that is not listed?

Choose “Other” when you run a check. The service is still checked, and the verdict is measured against the checker’s response-time threshold. No credit figure is calculated, because we do not invent one. You can still use the claim letter as evidence and read the credit tiers in your provider’s own SLA.

Credit is a percentage of the affected service's monthly bill, usually applied as a credit against future bills, not cash. Check the provider's SLA for exclusions.
