01

Run a check

Enter your service URL on the Check page and pick your provider. Add your monthly bill and the monthly uptime from your own monitoring if you want an indicative service credit calculated. Run a few checks: each result is a snapshot, not a measurement of monthly uptime.

02

Separate the check from the credit

The verdict describes the observed checks. A service-credit estimate uses your separate monthly uptime and billing inputs against the provider’s recorded credit tiers. The provider decides eligibility under its terms; a failed check alone does not establish a monthly SLA breach.

03

Download the claim letter

Copy the letter or download it as a file. It already contains the check evidence, the credit tier math, the source of the table, and the deadline. Add your account details before you send it.

04

File it with your provider

Send the letter through your provider’s support or billing channel (listed per provider below) and attach a Telegraph receipt only if you have a corresponding paid purchase. Free browser checks do not create one. Keep copies of everything, and file before the deadline: confirm the current claim window in the provider’s policy.

If the SLA was met

A passing check generates an honesty record instead of a breach claim. It records what the miner observed in that run. A passing snapshot does not rule out an incident at a different time; use the relevant monitoring period when reviewing eligibility.
