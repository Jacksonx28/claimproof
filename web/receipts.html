How verification works

Three steps between a claim and proof anyone can check.

01

The check is bought

A check is bought through the Telegraph engine and paid from escrow, at the miner’s listed price.

02

The engine issues a receipt

The receipt ties the miner’s answer to the miner identity above: who answered, what it cost, and when.

03

Anyone can match it

Match the receipt or signal ID in the list below against the engine record. That match is the proof attached to a claim.
