DownProof

Was your provider down? Find out what you're owed.

DownProof runs independent uptime checks against a standard SLA — the service must be up and answer within 1,000 ms — served live by the DownProof miner. If the SLA is breached, it works out the credit from the provider's own published table and drafts the claim. If the SLA is met, it says so. No fake claims.
