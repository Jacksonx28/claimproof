BEFORE YOU CHECK

Read the complete guide
Is this checker really free?
Yes. The web checker calls DownProof’s direct miner without a wallet or a charge. Paid Telegraph purchases are recorded separately in the proof ledger.
Does a failed probe guarantee a credit?
No. It records a point-in-time observation against the 1,000 ms threshold. Eligibility depends on the exact service, its policy, exclusions, and your monthly uptime records.
What happens if the service is healthy?
You receive an honest check record. DownProof does not draft a compensation claim when all samples meet the check threshold.
Can I monitor services over time?
The public watchlist shows the project’s configured nightly checks. These are free direct checks, not continuous monitoring or paid Telegraph receipts.
