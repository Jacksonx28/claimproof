Was your provider down? Find out what you’re owed.

DownProof runs independent uptime checks against a standard SLA (the service must be up and answer within 1,000 ms), served live by the DownProof miner. How it works

Three steps between “I think they were down” and a claim a provider can’t wave away.

01

We check

Independent live checks run through the DownProof miner on Telegraph, not your word against theirs: a third party’s measurement with a receipt.

02

We compare

The results are measured against the provider’s own published SLA credit tiers: their table, their commitment, their numbers.

03

You claim

You get a ready-to-send claim letter with the full proof pack and the claim-by deadline, or a signed honesty record when there’s no breach.

✓

No breach? No claim.

DownProof writes a signed honesty record instead: it talks users out of false claims, not just into real ones. Every check is on the record either way.

Providers covered

Credit tiers are taken from each provider’s published SLA. If a provider isn’t listed, DownProof still runs the check; it just won’t invent a figure.
